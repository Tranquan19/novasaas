01

Discovery

3-5 Days · Requirements & Validation

Every great product starts with a deep understanding of the problem. We work closely with you to define requirements, validate market fit, and analyze the competitive landscape. By the end of this phase, you'll have a clear, documented product specification.

  • Stakeholder interviews and goal alignment sessions
  • Market validation and competitor analysis
  • User persona definition and journey mapping
  • Feature prioritization and MVP scope definition
  • Technical architecture proposal and tech stack recommendation
  • Deliverable: Product Requirements Document (PRD)
02

Design

3-5 Days · UI/UX & Prototyping

With requirements locked, our design team creates wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes. We establish a complete design system — colors, typography, components — so your product looks and feels cohesive from day one. You'll review and approve every screen before we write a line of code.

  • Low-fidelity wireframes for layout and flow validation
  • High-fidelity UI mockups with real content
  • Interactive clickable prototype for user testing
  • Design system with reusable components and tokens
  • Mobile-responsive layouts for all breakpoints
  • Deliverable: Approved Figma prototype and design system
03

Build

7-14 Days · Agile Development with AI Acceleration

This is where our 8 AI agents shine. While a traditional team works sequentially, our Head of Product, Frontend Engineer, Backend Engineer, AI/ML Engineer, and others work in parallel — dramatically compressing development time. We run daily standups and deliver working features every 2-3 days.

  • Parallel AI-agent development across all stack layers
  • 2-3 day sprint cycles with working feature demos
  • Continuous integration with automated testing
  • Real-time progress tracking via Linear and GitHub
  • Daily Slack/email updates and weekly stakeholder reviews
  • Deliverable: Fully functional MVP on staging environment
04

Deploy

3-5 Days · Testing, CI/CD & Launch

Before going live, every feature goes through rigorous QA testing — automated and manual. We set up your production infrastructure, configure CI/CD pipelines, and ensure your application is secure, scalable, and monitored. You get a launch-ready product with zero technical debt.

  • Comprehensive QA testing (unit, integration, E2E, security)
  • Cloud infrastructure setup (AWS/GCP) with auto-scaling
  • CI/CD pipeline for automated deployments
  • SSL/TLS, monitoring, alerting, and backup configuration
  • Performance optimization and load testing
  • Deliverable: Live production MVP with monitoring
05

Iterate

Continuous · Learn, Improve, Grow

Launch is just the beginning. We help you measure user behavior, gather feedback, and prioritize what to build next. With our subscription plans, you get ongoing development, support, and strategic guidance — so your product keeps getting better.

  • Analytics setup and user behavior tracking
  • Feedback collection and feature request management
  • Bi-weekly or monthly product strategy sessions
  • Continuous feature development and improvement
  • Ongoing infrastructure maintenance and updates
  • Deliverable: Ever-improving product with growth roadmap
4-6 Weeks

From your first discovery call to a live, production-ready MVP. No delays, no surprises.
