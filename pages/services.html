Our Services

End-to-end SaaS development powered by AI. Every service is designed to get you from idea to revenue — fast.

Whether you need a full MVP built from scratch, AI capabilities woven into your product, or a white-label platform for your customers — NOVA delivers production-ready software in weeks, not months.

MVP Development

From a rough concept to a production-ready MVP in 4-6 weeks. We handle the full stack — frontend, backend, database, authentication, payments, and deployment. Our AI-accelerated workflow means you get more features, faster.

Each MVP ships with a modern tech stack, CI/CD pipeline, cloud infrastructure, and analytics built in. No boilerplate. No months of waiting. Just a working product you can put in front of users and investors.

  • Full-stack web application (Next.js, React, Node.js, Python)
  • Authentication, payments, email, and analytics integration
  • Cloud deployment (AWS/GCP) with CI/CD pipeline
  • Responsive, production-grade UI with design system
  • Starting at $2,000 — transparent, fixed-price quotes

AI Automation

Integrate cutting-edge AI into your SaaS product. We build RAG pipelines, intelligent agents, content generation systems, and workflow automation that give your users superpowers.

Our proprietary agent framework orchestrates multiple LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source) to handle complex tasks autonomously — from customer support chatbots to data extraction and report generation.

  • LLM integration (OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source models)
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines
  • Autonomous AI agents for workflow automation
  • Content generation, summarization, and data extraction
  • Custom fine-tuned models for domain-specific tasks

Strategy Consulting

We combine traditional market research with AI-powered competitive analysis to give you actionable insights. You'll walk away with a documented product strategy, technical architecture plan, and prioritized feature list.

  • Market validation and competitive analysis
  • Technical architecture and tech stack selection
  • Product roadmap and feature prioritization
  • Cost estimation and budget planning
  • Investor pitch deck and business model review
